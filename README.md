# Archimedes

**A modular business management platform.**
Archimedes is a software platform designed to simplify and organize business operations through a flexible, modular architecture.

The platform is built around two main concepts: **Sketches** and **Modules**.

## The Concept

### Sketches
Sketches represent different business segments, providing a foundation tailored to each type of business.

Examples include:
- Bakeries
- Confectioneries
- Restaurants and snack bars
- Retail stores
- Service businesses

### Modules
Modules provide additional functionality that businesses can select according to their operational needs.
The architecture is designed to allow modules to operate independently, offering flexibility and avoiding unnecessary dependencies.

## Our Vision
Our goal is to build an adaptable business management ecosystem where companies can choose the tools they actually need.
Rather than forcing every business into the same structure, Archimedes aims to provide a foundation that evolves alongside each operation.

## Project Status
**🚧Currently in development.🚧**

The initial development phase focuses on establishing the platform's foundations and validating its functionality with real businesses.
