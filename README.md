# Archimedes

**A business management platform built around flexibility.**

Archimedes is a project to build a platform that adapts to different businesses instead of making every business adapt to the same system.

Different businesses have different needs. A bakery doesn't operate like a phone store, and a small confectionery doesn't need the same tools as a supermarket.

Rather than building a completely separate system for every type of business, Archimedes brings them together through a shared foundation, specialized environments called **Sketches**, and optional **Modules**.

## How It Works

Archimedes is built around three concepts:
- **Core:** The shared foundation of the platform. It handles what different business environments have in common.
- **Sketches:** Business-specific environments, such as bakeries, confectioneries, snack bars, retail stores, and service businesses.
- **Modules:** Additional tools that businesses can choose according to their needs, such as inventory, employee management, CRM, scheduling, and purchasing.

When setting up an account, a business chooses its Sketch and then selects the modules it wants to use. The intention is to keep these choices flexible. A business shouldn't have to activate features it doesn't need just to use the ones it does.

## Why Archimedes?

The name comes from Archimedes, the Greek mathematician associated with the principle of the lever. The idea is often expressed through the phrase:

*"Give me a place to stand, and I shall move the Earth."*

That idea is at the heart of this project. A lever doesn't replace the force applied to it. It makes that force go further.

We see technology in the same way. The platform is the point of support, the modules are the levers, and the people running their businesses provide the knowledge and effort.

Archimedes should help them do more with what they already have — without adding unnecessary complexity to their work.

## Where We Want to Go

The first step is to build and validate a working version with real businesses. From there, the platform can grow with new Sketches, modules, and integrations.

We don't want to limit Archimedes to the business segments we can think of today. If a new type of business needs a different environment, the platform should be able to evolve to support it.

The goal isn't to build a system for every possible business right away. It's to create a foundation that makes those possibilities achievable over time.

## Project Status

Archimedes is currently in its early development and validation stage. The ideas and plans in this repository describe the intended direction of the project. They may change as we build, test, and learn from real use.
